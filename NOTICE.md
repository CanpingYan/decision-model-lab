# Decision Model Lab notices

The scenario designs started from the Apache-2.0 demos by Vishal Mysore (Copyright 2026 vishalmysore).

- [layaForWeb](https://github.com/vishalmysore/layaForWeb)
- [layaForWorkflows](https://github.com/vishalmysore/layaForWorkflows)
- [layaAsRagJudge](https://github.com/vishalmysore/layaAsRagJudge)
- [layaAgent](https://github.com/vishalmysore/layaAgent)
- [layaForMemory](https://github.com/vishalmysore/layaForMemory)

Decision Model Lab combines the scenarios in a new local HTTP app, with model adapters, Excel import, synthetic decision cases, scoring and export. It is an independent adaptation.

The Apache-2.0 text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The subset of the Chinese Decision Python package under `reference/chinese-decision/` comes from [yanqiangmiffy/chinese-decision](https://github.com/yanqiangmiffy/chinese-decision/tree/4708ede2c40e31fb8a851d58413c0e24e75f6326). Its [MIT license](reference/chinese-decision/LICENSE) and [upstream notices](reference/chinese-decision/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) apply to those files. The vendored subset contains the candidate processor, configuration and helpers needed for inference.

[Laya Multilingual](https://huggingface.co/convaiinnovations/laya-multilingual) and [Chinese Decision 0.8B](https://huggingface.co/quincyqiang/chinese-decision-0.8b) weights are fetched separately from their respective repositories under their model terms.
